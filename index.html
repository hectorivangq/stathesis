Research and Evaluation

Method

How a question becomes evidence you can defend.

I

Frame the question

We agree on the decision the evidence has to support, and the measure that will settle it, before any data is touched.

II

Design the method

Data sources, sampling, comparison groups and the analysis plan are written down in advance, so the result can't drift toward a preferred answer.

III

Test the evidence

Assumptions checked, small numbers protected, sensitivity tested. The interval narrows only as far as the data allow.

IV

Stand behind the finding

A clear report with its limits stated, reproducible methods, and a named senior statistician accountable for it.

Questions

Before you book.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Every service is available in English and Spanish, including reports, training and meetings.

Where do you work?

From San Juan, Puerto Rico, with clients in Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands, across the United States and in Latin America. Most work is remote, with site visits when a project needs them.

How are fees set?

Each engagement is quoted in a written proposal after the first consultation, based on its scope, timeline and the work involved.

Can you work with protected health information?

Yes, under a business associate agreement. We use de-identified data whenever we can, follow HIPAA security practices, and never ask for patient information through this website.

Can STATHESIS be the named evaluator on our grant?

Yes. We can prepare the evaluation plan and a letter of commitment for your application.

Does regulatory compliance work replace our attorneys?

No. It is analytical and operational work that complements your legal counsel.
